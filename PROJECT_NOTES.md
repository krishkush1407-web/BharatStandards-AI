# Judge Demo Flow
1. Home: search `100W LED street light, IP66, surge protection`.
2. Search & Recommendations: show ranked results and matched signals.
3. Standard Details: inspect requirements and certification signal.
4. Relevance Graph: show related standards.
5. Gap Detector: paste an incomplete tender and show missing clauses.
6. Tender Spec Editor: generate/edit a standards-aware specification and download it.
7. Upload: upload a PDF/DOCX/TXT tender and demonstrate extraction.

Pitch:
"The prototype separates extraction, normalization, retrieval, explainability, compliance-gap checks and drafting instead of using an ungrounded chatbot. In production, the local demo index is replaced by an authoritative BIS-indexed corpus and every recommendation is traceable to its source/version."
